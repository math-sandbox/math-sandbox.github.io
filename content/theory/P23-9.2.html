9.2 Position vectors

Based on Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics: Pure Mathematics 2 & 3 Coursebook (Sue Pemberton, series editor Julian Gilbey), Chapter 9 Vectors, printed pp.220–224. Own wording and new numbers, following the coursebook use of position vectors for distances, midpoints and dividing lines.

Key words: position vector, midpoint, unit vector

Position vectors

The position vector of a point \(A\) is the vector \(\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}\) from the origin \(O\) to \(A\). For any two points,

\[\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}.\]

The distance \(AB\) is the magnitude \(\lvert \mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}\rvert \).

Midpoints and other points on a line

The midpoint \(M\) of \(AB\) has position vector \(\dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b})\). If \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{BC}\), then \(B\) is the midpoint of \(AC\), so \(\mathbf{c}=2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}\).

More generally, write the vector you want as a route through known points, for example \(\overrightarrow{OC}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\overrightarrow{AC}\).

Worked example 1: Distance and midpoint

Coursebook, 9.2, position vectors. New numbers.

\(\mathbf{a}=3\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=5\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\). Find \(AB\) and the position vector of the midpoint of \(AB\).

  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=2\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+4\mathbf{k}\) B1
  2. \(AB=\sqrt{4+16+16}=6\) B1
  3. Midpoint \(=\dfrac{1}{2}(8\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j})=4\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}\) B1

Answer: \(AB=6\), midpoint \(4\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}\)

Worked example 2: A point dividing a line

Coursebook, 9.2, points on a line. New numbers.

\(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}\) and \(7\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}\). \(P\) lies on \(AB\) with \(AP:PB=1:2\). Find \(\overrightarrow{OP}\).

  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=6\mathbf{i}-6\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}\) B1
  2. \(\overrightarrow{OP}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\dfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}\) M1
  3. \(=3\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{k}\) A1

Answer: \(3\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{k}\)

Common mistakes

  • Writing \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}\). It is always end minus start: \(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}\).
  • Giving a position vector when a distance is asked for, or the other way round.
  • Forgetting to divide by the magnitude when a unit vector is required.

Past paper

9709/12/M/J/16 Q3, official QP and official MS.

Relative to an origin \(O\), the position vectors of points \(A\) and \(B\) are given by

\[\overrightarrow{OA}=2\mathbf{i}-5\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\quad\text{and}\quad \overrightarrow{OB}=4\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}.\]

The point \(C\) is such that \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{BC}\). Find the unit vector in the direction of \(\overrightarrow{OC}\). [4]

Mark scheme
  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=2\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}+4\mathbf{k}\) (or \(\overrightarrow{AC}=4\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}+8\mathbf{k}\)) B1 Official MS: B1, AB = 2i + j + 4k or AC = 4i + 2j + 8k.
  2. \(\overrightarrow{OC}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\overrightarrow{AC}=6\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+6\mathbf{k}\) M1 Official MS: M1, correct method for OC (alternatives: OC = 2OB − OA).
  3. \(\lvert \overrightarrow{OC}\rvert =\sqrt{36+9+36}=9\); divide \(\overrightarrow{OC}\) by 9 M1 Official MS: M1, divides by their modulus of their OC.
  4. Unit vector \(=\dfrac{1}{9}(6\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+6\mathbf{k})\) A1 Official MS: A1, correct unsimplified expression, (6i − 3j + 6k) ÷ 9.

Quick check 1

\(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\\5\end{pmatrix}\). Find \(AB\).

Worked solution
  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\\6\end{pmatrix}\)
  2. \(AB=\sqrt{4+9+36}\)

Answer: \(7\)

Quick check 2

\(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\begin{pmatrix}1\\4\\-2\end{pmatrix}\) and \(\begin{pmatrix}3\\0\\6\end{pmatrix}\). \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). Find \(OM\).

Worked solution
  1. \(\overrightarrow{OM}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\2\end{pmatrix}\)
  2. \(OM=\sqrt{12}\)

Answer: \(3.46\) (3 s.f.)

Quick check 3

\(\overrightarrow{OA}=2\mathbf{i}-5\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\), \(\overrightarrow{OB}=4\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{BC}\). Find \(\lvert \overrightarrow{OC}\rvert \).

Worked solution
  1. \(\overrightarrow{OC}=2\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}=6\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+6\mathbf{k}\)
  2. \(\sqrt{36+9+36}\)

Answer: \(9\)
