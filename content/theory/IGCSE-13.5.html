13.5 Exchanging currencies

Based on Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended Coursebook, 3rd edition (Karen Morrison and Nick Hamshaw), Chapter 13 Understanding measurement, printed pp.421–426 (Worked example 9 style on p.421, and later Apply your skills items). Printed pages were read from rendered scans of part_042 (printed 421–426). Own wording and new numbers.

Key words: exchange rate, currency, conversion

Using an exchange rate

A currency is the system of money used in a country, such as pounds, dollars, euros or yen. An exchange rate tells you how many units of one currency are equal to one unit of another. This is a conversion between two currencies.

Write the rate as a sentence first, then decide whether to multiply or divide. If \(£1=\$1.25\), then to change pounds into dollars you multiply by \(1.25\), and to change dollars into pounds you divide by \(1.25\).

Worked example 1: Changing pounds into dollars

Book: Worked example 9 style, printed p.421 (the book converts pounds into Botswana pula at a different rate). New numbers.

Change \(£80\) into dollars, given that \(£1=\$1.25\).

  1. \(£1=\$1.25\), so \(£80=80\times 1.25\) dollars. M1
  2. \(80\times 1.25=100\). A1

Answer: \(\$100\)

Worked example 2: Changing euros into pounds

Book: Apply your skills style on printed pp.422–425 (changing both ways). New numbers.

Change \(360\) euros into pounds, given that \(£1=1.20\) euros.

  1. \(£1=1.20\) euros, so to change euros into pounds, divide by \(1.20\). M1
  2. \(360\div 1.20=300\). A1

Answer: \(£300\)

Common mistakes

  • Multiplying when the rate is written the other way around, or dividing when you should multiply.
  • Leaving the answer in the original currency instead of the one asked for.
  • Rounding too early in a two-step calculation. In the examples here the rates give exact integer answers, so no rounding is needed.

Quick checks

Quick check 1

The exchange rate is \(1\) dollar \(=4\) rand. Change \(75\) dollars into rand.

Worked solution
  1. \(1\) dollar \(=4\) rand, so \(75\) dollars \(=75\times 4=300\) rand A1

Answer: \(300\)

Quick check 2

The exchange rate is \(1\) euro \(=1.25\) dollars. Change \(500\) dollars into euros.

Worked solution
  1. \(1\) euro \(=1.25\) dollars, so to change dollars into euros, divide by \(1.25\). \(500\div 1.25=400\) euros A1

Answer: \(400\)

Quick check 3

The exchange rate is \(£1=160\) yen. Change \(4800\) yen into pounds.

Worked solution
  1. \(£1=160\) yen, so to change yen into pounds, divide by \(160\). \(4800\div 160=30\) pounds A1

Answer: \(30\)

Marks: M = method, A = accurate answer (depends on M), B = independent mark. Leave exact values exact.
