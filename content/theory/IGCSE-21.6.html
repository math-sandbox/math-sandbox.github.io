21.6 Direct and inverse proportion in algebraic terms

Based on Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended Coursebook (Karen Morrison and Nick Hamshaw), Chapter 21 Ratio, rate and proportion. The wording is original and follows the published section titles and the standard Cambridge IGCSE treatment of the topic. Printed pp.745–753.

Key words: direct proportion, inverse proportion, constant of proportionality

Writing proportion with symbols

The symbol \(\propto\) means is proportional to. If \(y\propto x\) then \(y=kx\), where \(k\) is the constant of proportionality. If \(y\) is inversely proportional to \(x\), then \(y\propto\dfrac{1}{x}\) and \(y=\dfrac{k}{x}\).

The same idea works for powers and roots:

  • \(y\) is directly proportional to the square of \(x\): \(y=kx^2\).
  • \(y\) is directly proportional to the cube of \(x\): \(y=kx^3\).
  • \(y\) is inversely proportional to the square root of \(x\): \(y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}\).

Method

  1. Write the equation with \(k\).
  2. Substitute the given pair of values and find \(k\).
  3. Write the formula with the value of \(k\), then use it to find the unknown.

Graphs

The graph of \(y=kx\) is a straight line through the origin. The graph of \(y=\dfrac{k}{x}\) is a curve that gets closer to both axes but never meets them.

Worked example 1: Direct proportion with a cube

Coursebook, 21.6, direct proportion. New numbers.

\(y\) is directly proportional to the cube of \(x\). When \(x=2\), \(y=24\). Find \(y\) when \(x=3\).

  1. \(y=kx^3\), so \(24=8k\) and \(k=3\). M1
  2. \(y=3\times3^3=81\) A1

Answer: \(81\)

Worked example 2: Inverse proportion with a square root

Coursebook, 21.6, inverse proportion. New numbers.

\(t\) is inversely proportional to the square root of \(d\). When \(d=9\), \(t=8\). Find \(t\) when \(d=36\).

  1. \(t=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{d}}\), so \(8=\dfrac{k}{3}\) and \(k=24\). M1
  2. \(t=\dfrac{24}{\sqrt{36}}=4\) A1

Answer: \(4\)

Common mistakes

  • Using \(y=kx\) when the question says the square of \(x\), or forgetting the square when you substitute the second value.
  • Stopping after finding \(k\). Write the formula and answer the question.
  • For inverse proportion, multiplying by \(x\) instead of dividing when you use the formula.

Past paper

0580/21/M/J/20 Q16, official QP and official MS.

\(m\) is inversely proportional to the square of \((p-1)\).

When \(p=4\), \(m=5\).

Find \(m\) when \(p=6\). [3]

Mark scheme
  1. \(m=\dfrac{k}{(p-1)^2}\) M1 Official MS (as printed): M2 for m = k/(p − 1)², or M1 for m = their k/(6 − 1)²; OR M2 for 5(4 − 1)² = m(6 − 1)² oe.
  2. \(5=\dfrac{k}{3^2}\), so \(k=45\), and \(m=\dfrac{45}{5^2}\) M1 Official MS: M2 for the method with k found and used, or for 5(4 − 1)² = m(6 − 1)² oe.
  3. \(m=1.8\) A1 Official MS: 3 marks for 1.8 or 1 4/5.

Past paper

0580/22/O/N/23 Q22, official QP and official MS.

\(x\) is inversely proportional to the square root of \(w\).

When \(w=16\), \(x=3\).

Find \(x\) in terms of \(w\). [2]

Mark scheme
  1. \(x=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{w}}\) and \(3=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{16}}\), so \(k=12\). M1 Official MS: M1 for x = k/√w oe.
  2. \(x=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt{w}}\) A1 Official MS: 2 marks for x = 12/√w oe final answer.

Quick check 1

\(y\) is directly proportional to \(x^2\). When \(x=2\), \(y=20\). Find \(y\) when \(x=5\).

Worked solution
  1. \(20=4k\), so \(k=5\).
  2. \(y=5\times25\)

Answer: \(125\)

Quick check 2

\(y\) is inversely proportional to \(x\). When \(x=4\), \(y=9\). Find \(y\) when \(x=6\).

Worked solution
  1. \(k=4\times9=36\)
  2. \(y=\dfrac{36}{6}\)

Answer: \(6\)

Quick check 3

\(y\) is inversely proportional to the square of \(x\). When \(x=3\), \(y=5\). Find \(y\) when \(x=5\).

Worked solution
  1. \(k=5\times9=45\)
  2. \(y=\dfrac{45}{25}\)

Answer: \(1.8\)
