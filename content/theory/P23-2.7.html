2.7 Natural logarithms

Based on Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics: Pure Mathematics 2 & 3 Coursebook, Chapter 2, printed pp.42–43 (the natural logarithm, base e, and the laws of logarithms with ln). Printed pages were read from P23.pdf (printed 42–43 = PDF 54–55). Own wording and new numbers. Teaching stops before 2.8 Transforming a relationship to linear form on printed p.44.

Key words: natural logarithm, laws of logarithms

The natural logarithm of a positive number \(y\) is the logarithm to base \(e\), written \(\ln y\). The functions \(\ln x\) and \(e^x\) are inverses: \(e^{\ln k}=k\) and \(\ln(e^k)=k\), for \(k>0\) in the first identity. The domain of \(\ln x\) is \(x>0\).

The laws of logarithms hold with this base just as they do for any other valid base: \(\ln(xy)=\ln x+\ln y\), \(\ln(x/y)=\ln x-\ln y\), and \(\ln(x^m)=m\ln x\). A difference of natural logarithms is therefore a single logarithm of a quotient, and an equation such as \(\ln(x+a)-\ln x=\ln b\) becomes \((x+a)/x=b\), provided every argument is positive.

Worked example 1

Book: inverse work on printed p.42. New numbers.

Write down the value of \(e^{\ln 7}\) and the value of \(\ln(e^5)\).

  1. \(e^{\ln 7}=7\) and \(\ln(e^5)=5\). A1

Answer: 7 and 5

Worked example 2

Book: solving a two-term equation on printed pp.42–43. New numbers.

Solve the equation \(\ln(x+8)-\ln x=\ln 5\).

  1. The subtraction law gives \(\ln\bigl((x+8)/x\bigr)=\ln 5\), so \((x+8)/x=5\). M1
  2. Then \(x+8=5x\), so \(x=2\). This value is positive, so both logarithms are defined. A1

Answer: \(x=2\)

Common mistakes

  • Writing \(\ln x+\ln y\) as \(\ln(x+y)\) instead of \(\ln(xy)\).
  • Accepting a candidate that makes an argument zero or negative, so that a logarithm is not defined.
  • Treating \(\ln(x+1)-\ln x\) as \(\ln 1\), instead of \(\ln\bigl((x+1)/x\bigr)\).

Quick checks

Quick check 1

What is the value of \(e^{\ln 7}\)?

Worked solution
  1. The natural logarithm and the exponential function are inverses, so \(e^{\ln 7}=7\). A1

Answer: \(7\)

Quick check 2

What is the value of \(\ln\bigl(e^{5}\bigr)\)?

Worked solution
  1. The natural logarithm and the exponential function are inverses, so \(\ln\bigl(e^{5}\bigr)=5\). A1

Answer: \(5\)

Quick check 3

Solve the equation \(\ln(x+8)-\ln x=\ln 5\). What is the value of \(x\)?

Worked solution
  1. The subtraction law gives \(\ln\bigl((x+8)/x\bigr)=\ln 5\), so \((x+8)/x=5\). M1
  2. Then \(x+8=5x\), so \(x=2\). A1

Answer: \(2\)
